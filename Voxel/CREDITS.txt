VOXEL voices - credits

The singing voices were generated with:
- Piper TTS (https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl, GPL-3.0). Piper was only used as a tool
  to produce the audio; the plugin does not contain Piper code.
- The "en_US-libritts_r-medium" multi-speaker voice model, trained on LibriTTS-R
  (Koizumi et al. 2023, CC BY 4.0), which is derived from LibriVox public-domain audiobook
  recordings.
- The WORLD vocoder (pyworld, MIT) to re-synthesise every word at a steady sung pitch.

The voices are synthetic: they do not belong to, and should not be presented as, any real person.
